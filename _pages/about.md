I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Computer Systems Laboratory (CSL)](https://www.csl.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/) under the supervision of Prof. [Zhiru Zhang](https://www.csl.cornell.edu/~zhiruz/). I received my bachelor's degree in Microelectronic Science and Engineering from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/). I also have a minor degree in Financial Engineering from [PBC School of Finance (PBCSF) of Tsinghua University](https://eng.pbcsf.tsinghua.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie generally in the field of **domain-specific languages and compilers**. I'm particularly interested in improving computing efficiency by exploiting compressed data encodings and innovative data representations. My Ph.D. research mainly focuses on new abstractions and compiler optimizations for **efficient sparse data processing** on **heterogeneous hardware** such as GPUs and FPGAs.

In my leisure time, I enjoy hiking, photography, and rock climbing.
